The Vision

A Masterpiece of Plotting Architecture at Sultanpur Road Corridor

TRIIVANTA GOLD marks a signature residential township plotting development by KSGSK Infra Projects Pvt Ltd. While the grand vision encompasses a sprawling 45-acre township, Phase 1 focuses on 5 pristine acres of meticulously planned residential plots. The community is built around a wide 60 Ft. boulevard central layout, offering premium spatial freedom, clean streetscapes, and secondary 30 Ft. internal roads.

With a commitment to biophilic design, over half of the township is preserved as landscaped parks, green buffers, and open-air recreation spaces, ensuring your home is always surrounded by freshness and serenity.

Botanical Oasis

30,000 Sq. Ft. Central Green Park (Phase 1)

Located in the heart of Phase 1, our signature 30,000 Sq. Ft. botanical oasis is dedicated solely to Phase 1 residents. To maintain our high-end biophilic standards, even larger central parks and green recreation zones will be developed in the upcoming phases of this 45-acre township vision. It currently hosts a dedicated Jogger's Park, manicured flower avenues, sit-outs, and children's play meadows.
