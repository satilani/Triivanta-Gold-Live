## 1. Project Overview & Vision
* **Project Name**: TRIIVANTA GOLD
* **Developer**: KSGSK Infra Projects Pvt Ltd

* **Total Planned Scale**: 45-Acre Gated Residential Township (50%+ Preserved Open Area)
* **Phase 1 Development Scale**: 5 Pristine Acres (Comprising 38 Premium Plots, Central Green Park, and Shopping Zone)

## 2. Infrastructure & Premium Amenities (Phase 1)
* **100 Ft. Entrance Gate**: Architectural gateway arch with dual-fortified guardhouses and smart RFID visitor identity protocols(Proposed).
* **60 Ft. Main Boulevard**: Paved arterial boulevard with manicured tree linings, connecting to secondary 30 Ft. internal roads.
* **30,000 Sq. Ft. Central Green Park**: Multi-activity botanical green oasis containing:
  * Manicured walking paths & dedicated Jogger's Park
  * Landscape flower beds & wellness sit-outs
  * Safe children's play meadows
* **Dynamic Utilities**:
  * Complete underground storm water drainage network.
  * Underground electrical cabling (no overhead lines).
  * 24/7 security patrol, CCTV perimeter grid, and professional community governance.
* **Integrated Shopping Area**: Daily convenience commercial zone consisting of 6 convenience retail outlets earmarked for grocery, medical, cafe, and essential daily needs (Assumed).
